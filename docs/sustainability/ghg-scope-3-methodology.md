# CarbonFootprintTracker: GHG Protocol Scope 3 Calculation Methodologies

## 1. Executive Summary & Regulatory Framework

As modern knowledge work transitions towards distributed, hybrid, and remote-first operations, organizational greenhouse gas (GHG) accounting faces a paradigm shift. Traditional Scope 1 (direct combustion) and Scope 2 (purchased electricity in corporate headquarters) inventories fail to capture the decentralized emissions generated by employees commuting to third-party coworking hubs, cafes, and flexible workspaces.

WorkSphere's **`CarbonFootprintTracker`** and underlying **`carbonEngine.ts`** implement an automated, standards-compliant accounting pipeline based on:
1. **GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard:** Specifically **Category 7 (Employee Commuting)** and **Category 6 (Business Travel)**.
2. **EPA & EEA Emissions Factor Databases:** Calibrated lifecycle $\text{g CO}_2\text{e}/\text{km}$ factors for urban transit modes.
3. **ISO 14064-1 & EU CSRD (ESRS E1) Frameworks:** Providing auditable, itemized records suitable for enterprise ESG disclosures and annual sustainability reporting.

## 2. GHG Protocol Scope 3 Categorization

Under the GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Accounting and Reporting Standard:

| Scope 3 Category | Boundary Definition | WorkSphere Implementation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Category 7: Employee Commuting** | Emissions from the transportation of employees between their homes and worksites (including coworking facilities). | Automatically tracked for daily desk reservations, local hybrid commutes, and neighborhood hub sessions. |
| **Category 6: Business Travel** | Emissions from transportation for work-related activities in vehicles not owned/operated by the reporting entity. | Applicable when members travel inter-city or across regions to attend client summits or team off-sites. |
| **Category 8: Upstream Leased Assets** | Emissions from the operation of assets leased by the reporting company (e.g. rented enterprise flex suites). | Integrated via `isGreenCertifiedVenue` flag tracking renewable PPA and solar-powered facilities. |

## 3. Mathematical Formulations & Emission Factors

### 3.1 Mode-Specific Emission Factors ($EF_m$)

The emissions calculations rely on well-established EPA (US Environmental Protection Agency) and EEA (European Environment Agency) well-to-wheel (WTW) lifecycle intensity metrics:

$$\text{Emission Intensity Table } (EF_m)$$

| Commute Mode ($m$) | $EF_m$ ($\text{g CO}_2\text{e}/\text{km}$) | Methodology & Lifecycle Assumptions |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| `WALKING` | $0$ | Zero tailpipe and zero infrastructure energy allocation. |
| `BICYCLING` | $0$ | Human-powered transport; zero marginal emissions. |
| `ELECTRIC_SCOOTER` | $12$ | Micromobility charging lifecycle overhead from average national grid mix. |
| `PUBLIC_TRANSIT` | $35$ | Passenger-km blended average for urban heavy rail, light rail, and hybrid city buses. |
| `ELECTRIC_VEHICLE` (EV) | $48$ | Battery electric passenger car charging from standard utility grid ($0.42\,\text{kg CO}_2\text{e}/\text{kWh}$). |
| `GASOLINE_CAR` (ICE Baseline) | $172$ | EPA standard internal combustion passenger car ($25.7\,\text{MPG}$ equivalent). |

### 3.2 Commute Trip Emission Calculation

For any single workspace booking $i$ with one-way or round-trip distance $d_i$ (in kilometers) using transit mode $m_i$:

$$E_{\text{trip}, i} = d_i \times EF_{m_i} \quad [\text{g CO}_2\text{e}]$$

### 3.3 Counterfactual Baseline & Avoided Carbon ($\Delta E_{\text{avoided}}$)

To demonstrate the climate benefit of selecting sustainable mobility or localized coworking vs single-occupancy vehicle (SOV) driving:

$$E_{\text{baseline}, i} = d_i \times EF_{\text{GASOLINE\_CAR}} = d_i \times 172 \quad [\text{g CO}_2\text{e}]$$

$$\Delta E_{\text{avoided}, i} = \max\left(0, \, E_{\text{baseline}, i} - E_{\text{trip}, i}\right) \quad [\text{g CO}_2\text{e}]$$

### 3.4 Monthly Aggregates & Urban Tree Offset Equivalency

Aggregated metrics across a reporting period containing $N$ trips:

$$E_{\text{total\_emissions}} = \frac{1}{1000} \sum_{i=1}^{N} E_{\text{trip}, i} \quad [\text{kg CO}_2\text{e}]$$

$$\Delta E_{\text{total\_avoided}} = \frac{1}{1000} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \Delta E_{\text{avoided}, i} \quad [\text{kg CO}_2\text{e}]$$

#### Tree Carbon Absorption Equivalency ($T_{\text{trees}}$):
According to forestry carbon sequestration benchmarks, a mature urban tree absorbs approximately $21.7\,\text{kg CO}_2$ per year, which translates to $1.808\,\text{kg CO}_2$ per month:

$$T_{\text{trees}} = \frac{\Delta E_{\text{total\_avoided}}}{1.8} \quad [\text{Trees}]$$

## 4. Algorithmic Implementation in `carbonEngine.ts`

```typescript
export function calculateCommuteEmissions(
  distanceKm: number,
  mode: CommuteMode
): { emissionsGrams: number; avoidedGrams: number } {
  const actualFactor = EMISSION_FACTORS_G_PER_KM[mode] ?? 35;
  const baselineFactor = EMISSION_FACTORS_G_PER_KM.GASOLINE_CAR; // 172 g/km

  const emissionsGrams = Math.round(distanceKm * actualFactor);
  const baselineGrams = Math.round(distanceKm * baselineFactor);
  const avoidedGrams = Math.max(0, baselineGrams - emissionsGrams);

  return { emissionsGrams, avoidedGrams };
}
```

## 6. Auditability & Compliance Guidelines

1. **Activity Data Completeness:** Distance calculations utilize exact routing coordinates via Mapbox/OSRM turn-by-turn distances rather than radial as-the-crow-flies estimates.
2. **Double Counting Mitigation:** In hybrid workplace models, emissions are credited to the individual commuter and aggregated at the enterprise tenant ID without overlap with landlord Scope 1 building fuel combustion.
3. **Data Retention & Privacy:** Granular GPS origin addresses are never permanently retained in export logs; records store aggregate route kilometer lengths ($d_{\text{km}}$) and timestamped venue destinations to preserve member privacy.
